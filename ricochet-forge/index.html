RICOCHET FORGE

Privacy policy

LP App Studio · Last updated October 19, 2026

This policy applies to Ricochet Forge for Android, package com.lpcreative.ricochetforge, version 0.5.0. Earlier test builds used org.ricochetforge.game; the same local data practices described here apply to those builds.

Data used by the game

The current game does not collect or share personal data. It does not require an account, include advertising, billing or analytics SDKs, or send gameplay data to a developer server. The Android app does not request Internet access.

Local progress and settings

The game stores campaign progress, scores, virtual coins, player experience, unlocked equipment, an unfinished run, language preferences, and audio, vibration and visual-effect settings locally on your device. These records support gameplay and are not transmitted by the app. App data backup is disabled in the Android build.

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local progress. There is no in-game account or developer cloud-save service. You can manage local app data in Android's app settings.

Device permissions

The game uses Android's vibration permission for optional haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game's settings. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Advertising and purchases

Version 0.5.0 has no real advertising or in-app purchases. Virtual coins and equipment are part of local gameplay. If a future version adds advertising, purchases or other services that change data handling, this policy and the store disclosures will be updated before those changes are introduced.

Support and test feedback

If you choose to email us at Lperez848@gmail.com, your email address and the information you send are used to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information you do not want to share. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the same address, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Google Play and external links

Google Play handles store installation, testing-program participation and information you choose to submit through its services under Google's own privacy policies. This is separate from data stored locally by the game.

Opening this policy from the game launches an external browser. GitHub Pages hosts this policy and handles website connections under GitHub's privacy statement. The game does not transmit your progress to this website.

The Credits screen also provides optional external links to the original music sources, creators and license pages. Those sites handle website connections under their own privacy practices. The game does not add your local progress or profile to those links.

Updates and contact

This policy describes the current version. We will update it if the game's data practices change.
